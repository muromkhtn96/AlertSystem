01. Logic Hoat Dong

Zone la gi?

Zone = vung gia co xac suat cao gia se phan ung (bat len hoac bat xuong). Indicator phat hien zone tu 3 nguon:

  • Swing Point — dinh/day duoc xac nhan boi N nen moi ben
  • Order Block (OB) — nen institutional cuoi cung truoc impulse move
  • Wick Filter — thu hep zone vao vung liquidity grab

Chuoi Loc 7 Tang

Tu hang tram swing point, chi 3-6 zone Premium hien thi tren chart:

1
Swing Detection
Chi lay swing co N nen trai + N nen phai xac nhan (H1: N=5)
|
2
Momentum Confirmation
Gia phai di chuyen >= 20 pips (H1) sau swing — chung minh co reaction
|
3
ATR-Adaptive Proximity
Zone moi phai cach zone cu >= max(40 pips, ATR × 0.6). Zone manh hon 1.5x thay the zone yeu.
|
4
Zone Creation
OB detection → wick filter → ATR width cap (H1: 0.55×ATR) → FVG check → Imbalance check
|
5
Scoring (22 yeu to, 0-200 diem)
Chi zone Premium (≥120) hien thi mac dinh
|
6
Overlap Suppression
Zone chong cheo trong ATR×0.8 → giu zone manh nhat. Cung loai (2 support) → margin ×1.5
|
7
Level Toggle Filter
Mac dinh chi hien Premium. Bat them level neu can.

Multi-Touch Mitigation

ICT concept: Moi lan gia test zone, liquidity giam dan. Zone manh nhat khi chua test hoac chi test 1 lan.

FVG (Fair Value Gap)

FVG = khoang trong gia giua 3 nen (institutional imbalance). Zone overlap voi FVG cung huong: +15 diem. Nguoc huong: +5.

Breaker Block

OB bi pha boi impulse (body ≥ 0.8×ATR), sau do retest tu phia doi dien: +25 tong (15 reversal + 10 breaker).
